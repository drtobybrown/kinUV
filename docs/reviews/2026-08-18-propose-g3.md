# Generation-3 patches from chat-B review

Chat B’s ACK in `2026-08-18-review.md` was valid (four gates + two new residual risks). Clearing `pending` without writing those gates into ADRs left the canon stale. This propose folds them in as **`status: proposed`**. Do not treat gen-2 as closed until gen-3 is ACK’d.

OPS-AUTH had no new gate; it stays generation-2 `accepted`.

## What changed

1. **SPECRESP:** ≥1 native **guard channel** each side before Hann; trim after bin. Evaluate the model at `ν_edge ± Δν`, do not zero-pad visibilities (zero-pad biases outer bins if wings remain). `vel_buffer_kms` is an upper bound, not the minimum. New unit test: unpadded Hann fails an edge-line test.
2. **SB:** FFT pad **≥2× NAXIS** (066: ≥270; 512 default). **Not** tied to `ImageGrid.nx` (reviewer’s 256²@0.1″ coupling is rejected). `I ≥ 0` after iFFT. Flux-conserving resample if model cell ≠ 0.4″ (`|Δ∫I dΩ| < 1e-4`).
3. **PB + SHIFT:** `(dx, dy)` is an **image-plane** translation **before** `A(x,y)` at the phase centre. Visibility ramp after PB is forbidden (it drags the beam with the galaxy). Stationary-PB unit test at `(1″, 1″)`.
4. **OSCMETRIC:** `r_0 ≥ 0.5 BMAJ ≈ 0.65″`; `Δr ≈ 1.0–1.3″`. Mock suite must show `μ_mono = λ_reg` does not bias `V_0` low vs Stage A.

## Rejected alternatives

- Padding Ico FFT to `ImageGrid` 256²@0.1″: mixes Wiener grid with visibility Nyquist.
- Zero-padding the spectral axis instead of evaluating extra native channels.
- Keeping the gen-1 analytic ramp as the production `(dx, dy)` once PB is on.

## Residual risks (still open)

1. npz `(N, Δv)` unrecorded until 066-0.
2. `⟨w|V|²⟩` must be measured on the fit file.
3. 30 km/s restored Ico residuals at the 1709-pixel mask.
4. Clipping `I < 0` to 0 can drop a small fraction of Wiener ringing; flux is free so MAP absorbs it; catalogue-flux comparisons will not.
5. Image-plane `(dx, dy)` plus FINUFFT is slower than a ramp; still required for a correct PB.
