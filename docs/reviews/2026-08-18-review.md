## What Changed / What Was Checked

1. **SPECRESP:** Confirmed two-path separation. Native-grid convolution with $[0.25, 0.5, 0.25]$ before binning avoids broadening lines at binned resolution. Rigorous noise algebra confirms $Var(S) = (N - 0.75)\sigma^2$, yielding $s_{\rm theory}(8) = 12/29 \approx 0.4138$ and inter-bin correlation $\rho_{\rm bin} = 3/58 \approx 0.0517$. The empirical calibration $s = 2.0 / \langle w|V|^2 \rangle_{\rm line-free}$ is verified as the sole weight scale for 066 data fitting.
2. **SB:** Dimensionless SNR formulation $K = (\sigma_{\rm empty} / I_{\rm peak})^2$ corrects the dimensional inconsistency in generation 1. Rayleigh-Jeans conversion verified at $\nu_{\rm obs} \approx 224.17\text{--}224.3\text{ GHz}$ ($\sim 0.063\text{ Jy/K}$). Elimination of "multiply vis by $\tilde{B}$" prevents double-beaming.
3. **PB:** PB attenuation re-applied in image space prior to FINUFFT Type-2. FWHM formula corrected to $1.13 c / (\nu D_{\rm ant}) \approx 25.9''$ at $224.5\text{ GHz}$ for $D_{\rm ant} = 12\text{ m}$. Fixed pointing anchor at phase center prevents artificial kinematic warp fitting.
4. **OSCMETRIC:** Quadratic second-difference discrete regularizer correctly replaces Matérn-GP claims. Oscillation bound $\max_k \Omega_k < 0.3$ normalized to data $\Delta v_{\rm chan}$. Model selection via AIC ($\Delta \text{AIC} = 2 k_{\rm extra}$) properly avoids $N_{\rm vis} \sim 10^5$ BIC over-penalization.
5. **OPS-AUTH:** Single-job scope with $~30$-day certificate validation prior to `canfar create` prevents mid-run failures. Collision-free session naming format `kinuv-KGAS066-{sha6}-{map|nuts}` strictly stays within Skaha's 63-character limit.

## Attacks, Tight Bounds & Missing Gates Identified

### 1. SPECRESP: Spectral Edge Padding Gate (Missing Implementation Detail)
* **Attack:** Convolving the finite native channel range with $[0.25, 0.5, 0.25]$ without padding introduces truncation artifacts at channels $0$ and $N_{\rm native}-1$, corrupting the outer bins after binning.
* **Gate:** The forward line evaluator must evaluate at least 1 guard channel (or the full `vel_buffer_kms = 100.494` margin from `uvkin_settings.yaml`) on each spectral boundary before applying the Hanning kernel, and trim to the target binned channels only after the binning operator.

### 2. SB: Sky Zero-Padding & Positivity Enforcement (Missing Implementation Detail)
* **Attack:** Directly taking the 2D FFT of the $135 \times 135$ Ico map causes circular convolution wrap-around artifacts across boundaries. Additionally, Fourier division in noisy outer regions can produce unphysical negative surface brightness values.
* **Gate:** 
  1. Pad the $135 \times 135$ Ico map with zeros to at least $256 \times 256$ (matching `ImageGrid.nx, ny`) before computing the forward FFT for Wiener division, then crop back after inverse FFT.
  2. The reconstructed intrinsic template must enforce non-negativity $I_{\rm intrinsic}(x, y) \ge 0$ (clipping negative deconvolution residuals to 0) before quadrature node interpolation and flux normalization.

### 3. PB: Moving Galaxy vs. Stationary PB Envelope (Missing Unit Test)
* **Attack:** If the galaxy template is translated by $(dx, dy)$ after primary beam attenuation has been applied, the primary beam will incorrectly shift with the galaxy centroid rather than remaining stationary on the sky at the interferometric phase center.
* **Gate:** Add a unit test verifying that setting $(dx, dy) = (1.0'', 1.0'')$ shifts only the galaxy emission centroid relative to a fixed PB attenuation envelope centered at $(0, 0)$ in phase-center coordinates.

### 4. OSCMETRIC: Inner Ring Knot Anchoring (Tighter Bound)
* **Attack:** If radial rings are spaced uniformly down to $r=0$, the sub-beam innermost knot ($r < 0.6''$) will overfit beam-smearing gradients into an unphysical central velocity spike despite the curvature penalty.
* **Bound:** First ring knot must be placed at $r_0 \ge 0.5 \times \text{BMAJ} \approx 0.65''$, with subsequent knots spaced at $\Delta r \approx 1.0\text{--}1.3''$ across the $\sim 7.5''$ disk radius ($N_{\rm rings} = 6\text{--}8$).
