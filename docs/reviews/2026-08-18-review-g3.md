## Attacks, Tight Bounds & Refinements

### 1. SB: Centroid Shift via Non-Linear Clamping (Attack & Bound)
* **Attack:** Setting $I_{\rm intrinsic} = \max(I, 0)$ non-linearly zeros negative Wiener ringing and noise sidelobes. If background noise in the restored Ico map is asymmetric, positive-only truncation shifts the photometric centroid of the template away from $(0,0)$, which would alias into a spurious kinematic $(dx, dy)$ offset during MAP optimization.
* **Gate:** In the Gaussian fake Wiener unit test, assert that $I = \max(I, 0)$ does not shift the photometric centroid $\langle \vec{x} \rangle = \int \vec{x} I d\Omega / \int I d\Omega$ by more than $0.01''$ ($\le 0.1 \times \text{cellsize}$).

### 2. SHIFT: Sub-Pixel Image Translation Diffusion (Tighter Bound)
* **Attack:** Performing image-plane shifts $(dx, dy)$ via low-order discrete interpolators (e.g. bilinear) acts as a low-pass filter, blurring the intrinsic morphology before PB attenuation.
* **Bound:** Continuous image-plane shifts of $I_{\rm intrinsic}$ must use sub-pixel Fourier phase-shifting on the padded sky grid (or high-order spline interpolation) such that spatial profile broadening is $< 0.5\%$ at disk scale length $r_{\rm scale}$.

### 3. OSCMETRIC: Outer Knot Boundary Extrapolation (Missing Specification Gate)
* **Attack:** Quadrature nodes and sky grid pixels extending beyond the outermost knot $r_{\rm last} \approx 7.5''$ (e.g. out to the $10''$ CO mask boundary) can trigger polynomial extrapolation blow-ups if spline/ring boundary conditions are unconstrained.
* **Gate:** For all radii $R > r_{\rm last}$, the rotation curve must strictly evaluate as flat extrapolation $V_c(R) = V_c(r_{\rm last})$.
