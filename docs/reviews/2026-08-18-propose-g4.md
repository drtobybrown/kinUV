## What changed

1. **SB centroid gate:** `|⟨x⟩_clip − ⟨x⟩_signed| < 0.01″` absolute on the Ico WCS. Rejected “≤ 0.1 × cellsize” without naming the cell (0.4″ Ico → 0.04″, too loose vs σ(dx)=0.5″). **Fail closed:** if the real Ico fails the gate, skip hard clip.
2. **SHIFT interpolator:** Fourier phase shift on the padded grid (or cubic/quintic spline). Bilinear forbidden. Broadening `< 0.5%` in `r_scale` after a 1″ shift; also test 0.3″ and 2″.
3. **OSCMETRIC / VC evaluation BC:** `R > r_last` → `V = V(r_last)` (flat), rings only. **Challenge:** inner BC is **solid body** `V(R)=V(r_0)·(R/r_0)`, not flat `V(r_0)`. Stage A arctan is not flattened.
