## What Changed / Verification

1. **SB Centroid Gate:** Replaced ambiguous cell-relative threshold with an absolute $<0.01''$ shift limit on Ico WCS. The fail-closed fallback (skipping hard clip if real noise creates an asymmetric centroid bias) correctly prevents template-induced $(dx, dy)$ offsets.
2. **SHIFT Interpolator:** Explicitly bans bilinear translation; requires Fourier phase-shift on the padded grid (or cubic/quintic spline) with profile broadening $<0.5\%$ at $r_{\rm scale}$ across the $\pm 2''$ prior box.
3. **VC / OSCMETRIC Boundary Conditions:** 
   - Outer: $R > r_{\rm last} \implies V_c(R) = V_c(r_{\rm last})$ (flat, avoids extrapolation divergence in outer quadrature nodes).
   - Inner: $R < r_0 \implies V_c(R) = V_c(r_0) \cdot (R / r_0)$ (solid body through origin, eliminates central velocity jump).
   - Stage A (arctan) remains analytic across all radii.

## Attacks, Tight Bounds & Refinements

### 1. SB: Signed Template Normalization Gate (Attack & Refinement)
* **Attack:** When hard clipping is skipped due to centroid failure, $I_{\rm signed}$ retains negative Wiener ringing. If the normalization integral $\int_{\rm mask} I_{\rm signed} \, d\Omega$ is close to zero or negative due to anomalous noise, division by the integral causes a sign-inversion or divide-by-zero instability.
* **Gate:** Before normalizing an unclipped signed template, require $\int_{\rm mask} I_{\rm signed} \, d\Omega > 0.5 \int_{\rm mask} |I_{\rm signed}| \, d\Omega$, confirming that positive emission dominates total flux.

### 2. SHIFT: Fourier Shift Circular Wrap-Around Margin (Tighter Bound)
* **Attack:** Performing Fourier phase shifts on a padded grid wraps pixels that shift past the grid boundary around to the opposite edge.
* **Bound:** Padded grid boundaries must maintain a minimum buffer of at least $3 \times \sigma_{(dx, dy)} = 1.5''$ (or $\ge 15$ pixels at $0.1''$) beyond the outermost finite pixel of the 1709-pixel Ico mask to guarantee zero wrap-around power at the $\pm 2''$ prior boundary.

### 3. OSCMETRIC: Sub-Beam Solid-Body Assumption (Physical Bound)
* **Attack:** Solid-body extrapolation inside $r_0 \approx 0.65''$ imposes $dV/dR = \text{const}$. If a galaxy possesses an unresolved compact nuclear disc or central SMBH Keplerian rise, this forces a linear slope through $(0,0)$.
* **Bound:** For KGAS066 ($r_t \approx 3'' \gg r_0$), this assumption is physically sound and regularizing; for any future survey galaxy where central line width suggests a nuclear cusp, $r_0$ must be reduced with high-resolution data.
