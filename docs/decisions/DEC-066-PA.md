# Position angle

**Question:** How is PA treated for KGAS066?

**Answer:** Fit receding-side PA (E of N). Seed 205.2° from the CO YAML, not optical 108.9°. Moment-1 gives receding-side sign only. Photometric PA is not receding-side.
